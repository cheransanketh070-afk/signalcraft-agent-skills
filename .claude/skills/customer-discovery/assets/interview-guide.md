# Customer interview guide

## Opening
“Thanks for speaking with me. I’m researching how people handle [job/problem]. This is not a sales call. I’ll take brief notes; I won’t record or attribute quotes without permission. You can skip any question or stop at any time.”

## Questions
1. Tell me about the last time you needed to [job]. What triggered it?
2. What did you do first, and what happened next?
3. Which part took the most time, money, or effort?
4. What did you try before the current workaround?
5. What did you dislike or accept about the alternatives?
6. How often does this situation occur, and what happens if you do nothing?
7. Who else is involved in choosing or paying for a solution?
8. What would make you switch from your current approach?
9. Have you paid for a tool or service for this? What drove that decision?
10. What have I not asked that I should understand?

## Avoid
- “Wouldn't it be great if…?”
- “You would pay $X for this, right?”
- Treating compliments or hypothetical intent as purchase behavior.
- Recording names, employers, health details, or other identifiers that aren't needed.
