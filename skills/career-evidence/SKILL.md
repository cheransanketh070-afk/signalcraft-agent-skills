# Career Evidence

Improve relevance and clarity while preserving the candidate's actual history. Never invent experience, credentials, dates, metrics, employers, or skills.

## Workflow
1. **Gather source material.** Use the current CV/resume, project notes, role description, and any candidate-approved clarifications. Treat the candidate as the authority on their own experience.
2. **Extract role signals.** Identify must-have requirements, preferred qualifications, responsibilities, tools, domain language, and likely evaluation criteria. Separate explicit requirements from inferred preferences.
3. **Build an evidence map.** For each requirement, cite the candidate's actual project, task, action, result, tool, or credential. Mark gaps as unsupported, transferable, or needing clarification.
4. **Select evidence, not keywords alone.** Prioritize examples with scope, individual contribution, constraints, impact, and result. Quantify only with numbers supplied or validated by the candidate. Do not turn team outcomes into sole individual achievements.
5. **Tailor the document.** Reorder relevant experience, clarify transferable skills, use standard headings, and reflect key terminology naturally where truthful. Do not keyword-stuff or hide text to manipulate screening systems.
6. **Write achievement bullets.** Prefer action + context + method + outcome. When a metric is unknown, describe the observable outcome without fabricating a number.
7. **Protect consistency.** Keep titles, employers, dates, qualifications, and tool proficiency consistent with the source. Flag apparent date overlaps or contradictions for the candidate to resolve.
8. **Prepare interview stories.** Use STAR or CAR structure and distinguish the candidate’s actions from team activity. Prepare a concise answer for requirements where evidence is limited.
9. **Run an integrity review.** Highlight every claim that needs confirmation. Never recommend claiming a certification, degree, authorization, or work history the person does not have.
10. **Deliver a clear audit trail.** Provide a role-to-evidence matrix and identify any candidate follow-up needed before submission.

## Output package
- Tailored CV/resume or cover letter
- Requirement-to-evidence matrix
- Missing evidence / clarification list
- Keyword coverage (truthful only)
- Final consistency and readability check

## Example trigger
“Tailor my resume to this role. Keep every claim true, flag anything that needs confirmation, and show how each important requirement maps to my evidence.”
