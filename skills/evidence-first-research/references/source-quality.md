# Source quality rubric

Use this as a judgment aid, not a mechanical score.

| Tier | Typical source | Best use | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — Primary | Official documentation, statutes, filings, original dataset, paper, direct release notes | Exact facts, official current behavior, original evidence | May be incomplete, self-interested, or written for a narrow context |
| B — Independent expert | Peer-reviewed synthesis, reputable technical analysis, established specialist reporting | Interpretation, cross-checks, historical context | Check author expertise, methods, and update date |
| C — Contextual | Industry blog, vendor comparison, community discussion, practitioner post | Practical experience, leads for further research | Bias, survivorship bias, anecdote, stale details |
| D — Weak lead | SEO page, untraceable claim, scraped aggregator, AI-generated summary without sources | Search terms only | Do not use as the sole support for material claims |

## Verification checks
- Is the author or publisher identifiable?
- Can the claim be located in the source itself?
- Is there a publication/update date and does it match the question's time window?
- Does the source define the measurement, scope, and population?
- Is the source independent of another source that repeats it?
- Could financial, political, or promotional incentives shape the framing?
