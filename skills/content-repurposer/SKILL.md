# Content Repurposer

Multiply the usefulness of one source without turning it into repetitive, misleading, or generic content.

## Workflow
1. **Identify the source of truth.** Read the supplied transcript, article, video notes, product facts, or approved brief. Record its main claim, supporting evidence, intended audience, and rights/attribution constraints.
2. **Extract the content atoms.** Find one core insight, 3–5 supporting points, a concrete example, a useful tension or misconception, and the next step. Mark anything that is opinion, estimate, or unverified.
3. **Choose audience and channel.** Ask what the audience needs in that context: quick insight, explanation, discussion, action, or deeper reference. Respect platform character limits and format norms when known; verify current limits if they matter.
4. **Create native versions.** Make each output stand alone. Change the hook, pacing, evidence density, structure, and call to action for the platform instead of cross-posting the same text unchanged.
5. **Preserve truth.** Do not add statistics, quotes, testimonials, product features, or conclusions absent from the source. Use quotation marks only for exact wording. Keep links and attributions attached where needed.
6. **Protect people.** Remove private identifiers, unapproved personal stories, and confidential details. Do not imply a person endorsed a product without clear permission.
7. **Create a small batch.** Usually produce: one long-form outline, three short posts with different angles, one short-video script, and one newsletter/summary version—unless the user requests a different batch.
8. **Review for sameness and quality.** Each item must have a distinct angle, precise audience, useful payload, and clear CTA. Avoid engagement bait, invented controversy, fake scarcity, or claims of guaranteed virality.
9. **Add a publishing note.** Include source link, suggested asset, audience, CTA, and any claim that needs a fact-check before publishing.

## Output format
A table with: channel / angle / draft / CTA / source evidence / review note. Then provide a publishing sequence if useful.
