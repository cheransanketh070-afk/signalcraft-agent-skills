# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in SignalCraft welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Assume good intent while taking harm reports seriously.
- Share only information you are permitted to disclose.
- Respect the project's safety, privacy, and attribution rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or block participation for behavior that violates this code. Report concerns through the repository's private reporting channel or the contact method provided by the maintainer profile. Include links and relevant context without redistributing sensitive information.

This code is adapted in spirit from common open-source community conduct practices; it is not a substitute for applicable law or platform rules.
